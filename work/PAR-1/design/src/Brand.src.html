Paper & Ink.

The agreement is set in black type. Everything you fill in is blue ink. When Parley changes something, a highlighter shows you where.

01

The mark

%%MARK:128%%On paper
%%MARKD:128%%On dark
App icon and favicon

A pilcrow, the old sign for a new paragraph. Its bowl is a speech bubble, so the mark says what the product does: a conversation becomes a clause.

Wordmark: Newsreader 500 at −0.02em. Keep clear space equal to the bowl’s height on every side. Never rotate, stretch, outline or recolor the mark; on dark it switches to the dark palette.
